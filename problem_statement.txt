It's day two of a trade show. The booth staff have been standing for nine hours, their voices are gone, and the coffee ran out at noon. Meet Greeter: a robotic arm at the front of the booth that notices people walking by, turns to acknowledge them, gives them a moment of attention, and then moves on to the next passerby so the humans can sit down for a minute.

Greeter consists of:

a simulated robotic arm (Viam's builtin fake arm, or the simulated arm from the SO-101 module in the Viam Registry)
a webcam (your laptop camera or an external webcam)
a vision service running a pre-trained people detector

Using the Viam Python SDK, write a script containing a control loop that allows Greeter to:
Look for people using the camera and a machine learning model, and orient the arm toward them
Hold on a person for a moment so they feel acknowledged
Behave sensibly when nobody is walking by, and keep running when detections drop out or flicker
Attempt to not get "stuck" or crash when asked to move somewhere the arm can't reach

The arm and the camera don't share a physical world, so you will need to decide what "orient toward a person" means. Any reasonable interpretation is fine. In your README, state:

where you assumed the camera sits relative to the arm
how you map a detection in the image to an arm command, and why you chose that mapping
anything you would do differently on a real arm

Once your machine is configured, the 3D scene in the Viam app shows the simulated arm's current position and its movements, so you can see what your code is doing.
Other tutorials on the docs site may also be useful.

Detections come back as bounding boxes in image coordinates, and arm joint positions are read and written in degrees. The following prints the detections in the current camera frame and the arm's current joint positions:

from viam.components.arm import Arm
from viam.services.vision import VisionClient

# robot comes from a RobotClient connection
detector = VisionClient.from_robot(robot, "people_detector")
arm = Arm.from_robot(robot, "arm")

detections = await detector.get_detections_from_camera("cam")
for d in detections:
    print(d.class_name, d.confidence, d.x_min, d.y_min, d.x_max, d.y_max)

joints = await arm.get_joint_positions()
print(joints.values)
